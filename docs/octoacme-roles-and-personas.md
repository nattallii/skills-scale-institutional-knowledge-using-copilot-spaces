# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide engineering direction, align implementation with architecture decisions, and help the team deliver high-quality solutions while balancing technical trade-offs and delivery goals.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architectural decisions
- Review implementation quality and technical debt risks
- Support engineers through dependency resolution and technical decision-making
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and delivery readiness
- Help define technical standards, patterns, and validation approaches

### Goals
- Maintain a coherent, sustainable technical approach
- Improve delivery quality and reduce rework
- Enable teams to make informed technical decisions with clear ownership

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Engineering planning and dependency alignment
- Technical risk reviews during sprint and milestone planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to clarify technical direction and implementation trade-offs
- Partners with Product Managers on feasibility and delivery sequencing
- Coordinates with Project Managers to surface risks, dependencies, and milestone impacts
- Provides inputs to stakeholders when technical constraints or opportunities affect the roadmap

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners help identify, assess, and mitigate security, privacy, and regulatory risks throughout the project lifecycle so that delivery remains safe, compliant, and trustworthy.

### Responsibilities
- Review projects for security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Identify and escalate control gaps, vulnerabilities, and data-handling concerns
- Support secure design reviews and remediation planning
- Coordinate with stakeholders on required controls and approval gates
- Help define operational safeguards and incident response considerations

### Goals
- Reduce risk and strengthen trust in delivery outcomes
- Ensure compliance with internal standards and external obligations
- Prevent late-stage security issues that disrupt progress

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk assessments
- Compliance check-ins and control validation
- Escalation briefings during high-risk or incident-driven situations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure coding practices and review requirements
- Provides risk context to Product Managers and Project Managers when decisions affect scope, timing, or compliance obligations
- Works closely with Technical Leads to integrate secure design patterns into architecture
- Coordinates with stakeholders to align security expectations with release and operational readiness

---

## Customer Advocates / User Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Advocates represent end-user needs, feedback, and adoption considerations so the team builds solutions that are useful, usable, and aligned with customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer pain points, feedback, and usage data
- Translate user needs into clear product and workflow requirements
- Advocate for usability, accessibility, and service quality in prioritization decisions
- Validate that proposed solutions solve real customer problems
- Share feedback with cross-functional teams throughout the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and product adoption
- Keep delivery centered on real user needs
- Reduce the likelihood of building features that do not provide clear value

### Typical Communication
- User interviews, feedback reviews, and opportunity discovery sessions
- Product prioritization and roadmap alignment conversations
- Validation loops during pilots, releases, and learning reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supplies insight to Product Managers for prioritization and success metrics
- Helps Project Managers communicate stakeholder impact and adoption considerations
- Provides user context to Developers and Technical Leads when designing and testing experiences
- Supports release readiness by validating that outcomes meet customer expectations

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness, rollout planning, and stakeholder communication so changes are delivered in a controlled and observable way.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules, checkpoints, and communications
- Validate readiness against acceptance criteria, deployment checklists, and rollback plans
- Align engineering, support, and stakeholder teams around launch timing and responsibilities
- Monitor release health and support quick issue escalation if needed
- Document release outcomes, follow-up actions, and learnings

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and avoid avoidable release disruption
- Ensure clear communication before, during, and after release events
- Support confident, repeatable delivery of new capabilities

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness reviews
- Deployment notifications and stakeholder updates
- Post-release validation and incident follow-up discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers and Project Managers on launch timing and communications
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads to confirm readiness, rollback options, and deployment quality
- Coordinates with Customer Advocates to understand user impact and support readiness expectations
- Supports Security and Compliance Partners by confirming controls and incident communication plans are in place

---

## Data / Analytics Partners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Partners help teams measure outcomes, validate assumptions, and assess the impact of initiatives through clear metrics and evidence-based reporting.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Analyze trends, usage, and experiment results
- Support decision-making with objective evidence and operational insights
- Help identify gaps in measurement and reporting readiness
- Collaborate on post-release evaluation and continuous improvement activities

### Goals
- Make outcomes measurable and understandable
- Improve the quality of decisions using evidence rather than assumptions
- Support learning loops that strengthen project outcomes over time

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and reporting sessions
- Measurement planning and experimentation discussions
- Post-release impact reviews and performance updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on metric definitions and outcome validation
- Provides evidence to Project Managers for status reporting and risk assessment
- Helps Developers and Technical Leads assess system performance or quality signals
- Supports Customer Advocates and stakeholders by clarifying how outcomes are tracking against user value and business goals

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These role definitions help clarify accountability, decision ownership, and collaboration across planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement.
